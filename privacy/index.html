Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

We collect only what we need, use it only for the reason you gave it to us, and never sell it.

1. Who we are

Ms. Madeleine Cafe L.L.C. ("Ms. Madeleine", "we", "us") is a company in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We run this website, msmadeleine.com.

Contact for anything in this policy: connect@msmadeleine.com, Penrose Place, Jumeirah Village Circle, Dubai.

We follow the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).

2. What this policy covers

This website, and job applications you send us through it or by email.

3. What we collect

WhenWhat
You apply through the designer formName, email, WhatsApp number (optional), where you are based, your portfolio link and/or PDF portfolio, a food brand link (optional), your price, the days you need, your note, and that you agreed to this policy.
We shortlist you and you book a callThe name and email you enter on our booking page, the time you pick, and our notes from the call.
You email usYour email address, what you write, and any files or links you send.
You visit the websiteWe use no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Our web host records basic technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and secure.

4. Why we use it

  • To review your application and portfolio.
  • To contact you about this hiring round.
  • To choose a designer and, if we hire you, to make and run the agreement.

We use your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you tick the box on the form or send us your application. We do not use it for marketing.

5. Who sees it

Only the Ms. Madeleine partners who make the hiring decision. We also use a few service providers to run the website and our email:

ProviderWhat it does for us
Google (Sheets, Drive, Apps Script, Calendar, Meet)Receives the form, keeps applications in a private Google Sheet and PDF portfolios in a private Google Drive folder, sends us a short notice by email, and runs the booking page and video calls for shortlisted designers.
ResendSends our emails about your application (shortlist, brief and decision) from connect@msmadeleine.com.
Microsoft 365Our email, where we get notices and write to applicants.
GitHub PagesHosts this website.
CloudflareRuns our domain name records.

Some of these providers handle data outside the UAE. We never sell or rent your data, and we never share it for anyone else's marketing.

6. How long we keep it

  • If we do not hire you: we delete your application, including any PDF you uploaded, within 6 months after we choose a designer.
  • If we hire you: we keep what the work and our business records need, for as long as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • see the data we hold about you;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • stop using it, or withdraw your consent.

Email connect@msmadeleine.com. We reply within 30 days. Withdrawing consent removes you from the hiring round.

8. Your work

Designs, plans and palettes you send us stay yours. If we do not hire you, we do not use them, or anything close to them.

9. Keeping it safe

Only the people making the decision can open applications. The Sheet and the PDF folder are private, and our accounts are protected by Google and Microsoft 365 security. No method is perfect, so please do not send us passwords, ID numbers or bank details. We never ask for them.

10. Age

This hiring round is for adults. Please do not apply if you are under 18.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top.
