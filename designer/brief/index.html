Who we are

A cake shop built around one feeling.

The joy of seeing something beautiful, and finding it tastes even better.

There's a moment when you see a dessert so pretty, it stops you for a second. Then you take a bite, and it tastes even better. That's the moment we bake for at Ms. Madeleine. Our chefs learned pastry in professional kitchens, where they found a quiet joy in every batch that comes out just right. But the best part is yours: the "wow" when you see it, and the smile when you taste it. Desserts you know, and a few you're about to love, all done right. Lovely to look at. Lovelier to taste.

Why the name. Of everything we bake, madeleines gave us that feeling the most. When a tray comes out and every little bump has risen just right, we still smile. So we named the shop after them.

Why us. Most places give you one of the two joys. We give you both. Pretty shops often taste dry or too sweet, and properly made ones often look plain.

What we serve

Classic desserts, finished beautifully.

At the counter

  • Vintage piped whole cakes, 4 and 6 inch. The 4 inch is our signature.
  • Slices: fraisier, carrot, chocolate with whipped caramel, honey cake, Basque cheesecake.
  • Individual desserts: Paris-Brest, two entremets, raspberry cheesecake, tiramisu cup.
  • Madeleines, financiers, lemon loaf and shortbread. Madeleine gift boxes.

Coffee and tea

  • Espresso drinks, hot and iced, and three rotating signature lattes.
  • Tea in a vintage pot, matcha and hot chocolate.
  • A small shortbread with every hot coffee.
  • Slices cost AED 30 to 40 and a cappuccino AED 24: above everyday cafés, below the mall trend brands.

Beyond the shop

Celebration and wedding cakes, dessert tables for events, and cakes for cafés and businesses. Most of our orders will come from here, so the brand has to work for brides, event planners and companies too, not only for walk-ins.

A delivery-only brand

We also run a dessert brand sold only on delivery apps, under a different name. It needs a mini identity: a logo, fonts and colours. We supply the name.

Where and who

A small shop with a lit display.

  • Penrose Place, Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC), Dubai. About 450 sq ft for guests and only a few tables. The front is mostly glass, so the lit cake display shows to the street. We open in December 2026.
  • The display is a glass showcase in the style of a Tokyo patisserie, with a few vintage whole cakes. Not fully Tokyo, not fully Parisian.
  • An interior designer is designing the shop. The brand and the interior must work together, so you will share files with them.
  • Who comes: trend-aware Dubai, looking for something new and pretty and happy to pay AED 40 for a slice (they already go to places like L'ETO, Brunch & Cake, EL&N and Cherryhouse). Regulars who sit alone with a coffee and a slice, or grab and go. Families celebrating. Brides and wedding planners. Cafés and businesses that buy cakes from us.

Look and feel

The desserts are the jewellery.

Beauty with brains. Silently loud and confident.

  • Quietly confident
  • Feminine
  • Elegant
  • Calm and uncluttered
  • Modern, with a vintage touch
  • Everything else stays quiet so the desserts shine. Very uncluttered.
  • Colour: mostly cream and off-white, with one quiet, muted accent. Choosing that accent is part of your first take.
  • Materials: marble and brass, softened with a little fabric. Warm, soft light. Vintage china that looks like jewellery.
  • Voice: elegant, with few words. Our staff are warm and chatty and remember people.
  • How guests should leave: thinking "that was beautiful" and "I felt welcome".

Not us

  • A pastel or colourful cake-shop look, or a cheap bake sale.
  • A jewellery boutique. The cakes are pretty and feminine; the rest stays simple.
  • An invented "Ms. Madeleine" character or mascot, or anything cheesy or forced.
  • A brand built around the madeleine. The name comes from it, but cakes are the main character.
  • "Neighbourhood cake shop". We are a fancy one.

Good to know before you start

The practical bits.

  • The name is "Ms. Madeleine", spelled exactly like this, with the dot. Our registered trade name is "Ms. Madeleine Cafe", in Arabic مس. مادلين كافيه. The full registered name must appear once outside the shop; the logo itself can say "Ms. Madeleine".
  • Every logo needs an Arabic version, for the shopfront and more.
  • A branded square board sits under every mini dessert, from the display to the takeaway box. You decide its colour, logo placement and final size (our working size is about 9 × 9 cm). One takeaway box fits the board. No clear plastic slice boxes.
  • No single-use plastic. Dubai bans single-use plastic cups, lids, plates, food containers and bags from 1 January 2026. Design for paper and other allowed materials.
  • Two Instagram accounts: one for the shop's pastries, one for custom orders and catering. Our in-house team does the photos and posting.
